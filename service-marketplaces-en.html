Ozon and Wildberries: we analyse the sales funnel, improve product listings and identify what stands between discovery and purchase.

Funnel diagnostics · account audits · listing SEO · content & design

A clear plan, backed by data

You receive a prioritised action plan, findings supported by data, and metrics to evaluate the changes. Start with one listing or expand the work to a product group or account.

Find where buyers drop off

We trace impressions, clicks, add-to-cart actions, orders and completed purchases. We identify the weakest stages and separate content issues from price, stock, delivery and advertising effects.

Audit the account, not just one listing

We review sales by SKU and category, competitors, ratings, reviews and questions. We examine internal ad spend, advertising cost as a share of revenue, and unit economics where data is available.

Make listings easier to find

We research search queries and competitors, then refine titles, attributes and descriptions. We align the listing with buyer intent and check visibility and conversion before and after changes.

Turn product features into a clear story

We structure the cover image, gallery and copy around buyer questions and reasons to choose your product. We create design briefs and ready-to-publish assets using the materials you provide.

Prioritise changes and measure the effect

We agree on the scope and baseline metrics, then track click-through rate, add-to-cart conversion and downstream sales. We compare equivalent periods and account for promotions, seasonality and availability.

Bring category context to the analysis

Our beauty and cosmetics experience helps us look closely at product positioning, stated benefits, usage and customer concerns. Case studies are available on request; recommendations are tailored to your category.
